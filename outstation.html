About Our Outstation

Our History

St. John of the Cross Outstation was established in 2005 to serve the growing Catholic community in Elebu and surrounding areas. We began with just 20 families and have grown to over 150 families today.

Read More

Our Patron Saint

St. John of the Cross was a Spanish Catholic priest, mystic, and Doctor of the Church. He is known for his reforms and writings on spiritual growth and the dark night of the soul.

Learn More

Our Mission

To nurture a vibrant Catholic community through worship, education, service, and fellowship, following the example of Jesus Christ and our patron saint.
